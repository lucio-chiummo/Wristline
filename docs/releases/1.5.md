**Ring by notification.** For watches that lose the Answer button: Wristline can ring the watch through a notification instead. It offers this after a test ring nobody answered, and you can switch it on in Settings. Answering on the watch still sends the voice to the watch.

**A full-screen incoming call on the watch.** While the phone rings, Wristline's watch app shows Answer and Decline full screen and vibrates for the whole ring. After Answer it turns into Wristline's call screen (timer, mute, hang up). On Pixel watches, a double pinch answers or hangs up and turning your wrist declines.

**Voice stays on the watch on Android 17 and HyperOS.** On phones where the calling app kept pulling the voice back to the phone (Xiaomi/HyperOS, Android 17), Wristline now relays the call as a managed phone call. Turn on "Wristline — voice on watch" from the card Wristline shows; from then on the voice stays on the watch. Your phone's dialer shows a Wristline call while a relayed call is on. That's expected.

**Fixes.**
- The watch stops vibrating when you answer, and the ringtone no longer plays into the answered call.
- Hanging up on the watch also ends the call on the phone, even when the phone falls back.
- A second Answer or a late Decline is ignored.
- Calling out from the watch is refused while a relayed call is on, and the watch says so.

**A fuller diagnostic report.** The report now includes the watch model and app version, the phone's call audio route and Bluetooth voice link, and every call Wristline starts and why. It also includes Android's own log of the call's audio when Wireless debugging is on. Personal data is masked. Read the report before posting it publicly.

Install both APKs (phone and watch) from this release.
