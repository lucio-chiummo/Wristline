**Fix: hanging up from the watch no longer calls the person back.** After the other person hangs up or doesn't answer, WhatsApp keeps its call notification with a "Call again" button. If you then hung up from the watch, Wristline could press that button and start a new call. Wristline now never presses "Call again" (in any language) as hang-up, and recognises "Cancel" as hang-up.

Install both APKs (phone and watch) from this release. They update 1.5 in place.
